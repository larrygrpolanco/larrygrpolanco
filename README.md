
# Hi, I'm Larry

I'm a researcher and developer with a background in applied linguistics and education, currently working in cancer research at Moffitt Cancer Center.

My work has taken me from studying vocabulary in academic lectures to evaluating LLM reliability, developing educational software, and analyzing clinical data. I enjoy research, programming, and building things I find useful or interesting.

My GitHub contains my independent projects, research code, and experiments. Most of my work in medical research isn't publicly available here.

## Selected Projects

### Research and Research Tools

- **[LLM Temperature Consistency](https://github.com/larrygrpolanco/llm-temperature-consistency)** — Code and replication materials for my published study on LLM annotation reliability. The experiment involved 18,000 runs and over 450,000 sentence annotations to examine how sampling temperature affects consistency and classification performance.

- **[YASEC](https://github.com/larrygrpolanco/youtube-transcript-corpus-study)** — A 58-million-word corpus of academic YouTube lectures developed for my vocabulary research, later published in the *International Journal of Applied Linguistics*. Also my first substantial programming project.

- **[Thyroid Pathology LLM Abstraction](https://github.com/larrygrpolanco/TCGA-THCA-pathology-llm-abstraction)** — A public evaluation of seven models for extracting structured information from thyroid pathology reports in TCGA-THCA.

- **[SABR Automated Coding](https://github.com/larrygrpolanco/SABR-automated-coding)** — An experimental workflow for qualitative coding of educational transcripts using LLMs, with evaluation against human coding.

### Applications

- **[Language Learning Journal](https://github.com/larrygrpolanco/language-learning-journal)** — A notebook-based language-learning app I've been building for myself. It combines journaling, vocabulary collection, and several forms of practice using material from my own learning.

- **[Taped Chinese](https://github.com/larrygrpolanco/standard-chinese-platform)** — A digital adaptation of the Foreign Service Institute's *Standard Chinese* course, with its original audio materials, a cassette-inspired interface, and interactive exercises.

- **[ITA Trainer](https://github.com/larrygrpolanco/ita-trainer)** — A voice-based simulation for international teaching assistants to practice classroom communication and receive feedback. Developed in collaboration with education researchers.

- **[Jihye's Korean Class](https://github.com/larrygrpolanco/jihye-learning-platform)** — A website built for my partner's Korean class, including course resources, Hangul activities, and a small pixel-paint app.

- **[Fulbright Planner](https://github.com/larrygrpolanco/eta-lesson-planner)** — A lesson-planning application I developed during my Fulbright year in Taiwan. Tested with approximately 50 teaching assistants and refined using their feedback.

- **[Office Agent Simulation](https://github.com/larrygrpolanco/office-agent-simulation)** — An experiment with simulated agents that perceive, plan, reflect, and interact in a virtual office, drawing on the *Generative Agents* paper.

Other projects and experiments are available in my [repositories](https://github.com/larrygrpolanco?tab=repositories).
